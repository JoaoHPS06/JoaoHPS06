### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Computer Science undergraduate** (7th semester) at **UFOP – Federal University of Ouro Preto**, Brazil, working at the intersection of **AI** and **immersive technology**.

- 🔭 Currently researching **immersive VR educational systems** with Unity 3D for a standalone headset (Meta Quest) at UFOP's Pharmacy Museum
- 🤖 Building **LLM-powered applications** and evaluating **computer vision foundation models** (SAM, Cellpose)
- 🏗️ Passionate about **distributed systems**, backend engineering and applied AI
- 🌎 Fluent in **English**, intermediate **Spanish**
- 📬 Open to **internship and junior roles** in AI Engineering, Backend or XR

### 🔬 Active Research

**Immersive Virtual Reality for Cultural Heritage — UFOP Pharmacy Museum**
> Serious game built in Unity 3D for a standalone VR headset (Meta Quest), recreating a 19th-century pharmacy laboratory so visitors can explore the space and manually interact with 3D replicas of historical instruments. Developed with a Participatory Design methodology involving curators, staff and visitors as active agents in the design process.
